## Iteration 1 — Launch Scenarios

- Launching a robot with a valid command succeeds
- Launching a robot with an invalid command fails
- Launching a robot into a world with no free space fails
- Launching a robot with a duplicate name fails

## Iteration 2 — Launch Scenarios

- Launching multiple robots into a world with free space succeeds
- Launching a robot into a world with an obstacle and free space succeeds
- Launching a robot into a world with an obstacle and no free space fails
- Launching a robot into a world with no obstacle but no free space fails
